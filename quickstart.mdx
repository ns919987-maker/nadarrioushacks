Once you have NitroType Cheat v2 installed, completing your first automated race takes just a few steps. This page walks you through opening the app, logging in, setting up your configuration, and letting the cheat handle a full race from start to finish.

## Run Your First Automated Race

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Launch NitroType Cheat from your desktop shortcut or Start Menu. The app window will open and the embedded browser will automatically navigate to [NitroType.com](https://nitrotype.com) — no need to open a separate browser tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to NitroType">
    Inside the embedded browser, sign in to your NitroType account as you normally would. Your credentials are entered directly into the NitroType website and are never stored or read by the cheat app itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your settings">
    On the left side of the app, you will find sliders and checkboxes that control how the cheat behaves. For your first run, the defaults are a perfectly fine starting point — **Auto Start** is enabled by default, and the typing rate and accuracy sliders are pre-set to reasonable values.

    If you want to adjust anything before your first race:

    - **Typing Rate** — controls how fast the cheat types, measured approximately in WPM. Drag the slider left to slow down or right to speed up.
    - **Typing Rate Variance** — adds a random offset to each keystroke delay, making timing less mechanical.
    - **Accuracy** — sets the percentage of characters typed correctly. Lower values introduce deliberate mistakes.
    - **Accuracy Variance** — adds a random offset to the accuracy calculation each race.
    - **Auto Start** — when checked, the cheat detects the race text automatically and begins typing without any manual input from you.

    <Tip>
      For your first run, keep **God Mode** off and set the typing rate to somewhere around 100–150 WPM. This range is much closer to a fast human typist and significantly reduces the risk of your account being flagged or banned.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join a race">
    In the embedded browser, navigate to a race lobby on NitroType as you normally would — click the race button from your garage or team page to queue for a race.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the cheat do the work">
    Once the race starts and the typing prompt appears on screen, the cheat takes over:

    - **If Auto Start is enabled** (the default), the cheat detects the race text automatically and begins simulating keystrokes without any action from you. Just wait.
    - **If Auto Start is disabled**, click the **Start** button in the app once the race has begun and the typing prompt is visible. The cheat will read the race text from the page and begin typing immediately.

    You will see the in-game typing field fill in as if someone were typing at the keyboard. The race plays out to completion and your results are recorded normally on NitroType.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the Race

Once the cheat finishes typing the last character, the race ends and your results are submitted to NitroType as usual.

If you have **Auto Game** enabled, you do not need to queue for the next race yourself. After a short randomized delay (between roughly 4.25 and 4.5 seconds), the cheat automatically sends an Enter key press to advance you into the next race. This means the app can run through back-to-back races continuously without any input from you.

<Note>
  Your settings — including typing rate, accuracy, Auto Start, Auto Game, and Use Nitros — are saved automatically every time you close the app. The next time you open NitroType Cheat, all of your preferences will be restored exactly as you left them, so you never need to reconfigure from scratch.
</Note>
