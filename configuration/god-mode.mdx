God Mode is a special toggle that removes every constraint on the cheat's typing engine. Instead of waiting between keystrokes, it fires them as fast as the browser can process input — while simultaneously locking accuracy to a perfect 100%. The result is the absolute maximum WPM your system and browser can sustain, with zero errors.

<Warning>
  God Mode makes your performance **extremely obvious** to NitroType's anti-cheat systems. Finishing races at thousands of WPM with perfect accuracy is far outside any human range and is very likely to trigger an account ban. Enable it only on accounts you are willing to lose, and use it at your own risk.
</Warning>

## What God Mode Does

God Mode changes two core behaviors in the typing engine:

- **Overrides Typing Rate.** Instead of waiting the number of milliseconds set by your Typing Rate slider, the cheat dispatches each keystroke as rapidly as the browser's input pipeline will accept them — effectively no delay between characters. In practice, keystrokes are sent as fast as your system and browser can process them.
- **Forces Accuracy to 100% for the race.** No intentional mistype errors are inserted during the race, regardless of what the Accuracy slider is set to. Your Accuracy slider value itself is not changed — it is simply ignored while God Mode is active, and your setting is restored the moment you turn God Mode off.

Under optimal conditions — fast hardware and a responsive browser — God Mode can reach speeds approaching **~4,000 WPM**, though actual throughput varies depending on your system performance and browser processing latency.

## How to Enable God Mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the God Mode checkbox">
    In the NitroType Cheat v2 app, locate the **God Mode** checkbox and click it. A confirmation dialog will appear warning you that God Mode removes all typing limits and that your account is highly likely to be banned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in the dialog">
    Click **Yes** in the warning dialog to confirm. God Mode is now active. If you click **No**, the checkbox returns to unchecked and nothing changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disable God Mode, simply uncheck the **God Mode** checkbox. No confirmation dialog is shown when turning it off — it deactivates immediately.

<Note>
  God Mode overrides both the Typing Rate and Accuracy sliders **for the duration of each race** while it is active, but it does **not** change your slider values. As soon as you uncheck God Mode, the cheat resumes using whatever Typing Rate and Accuracy you had configured.
</Note>
