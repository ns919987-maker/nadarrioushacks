The Accuracy slider controls what percentage of characters the cheat types correctly. Rather than always producing a flawless race, NitroType Cheat v2 can intentionally insert mistype errors at a rate you choose, making your performance look like that of a real, imperfect human typist. Pairing this with a reasonable typing speed is one of the best ways to avoid standing out.

## Accuracy

The slider accepts values from **0 to 100 percent**. The default is **100%**, which means no errors are inserted and every character is typed correctly.

When you set accuracy below 100%, the cheat calculates exactly how many errors to insert before the race begins using this formula:

```
letters_to_miss = floor(race_text_length × (100 − accuracy) / 100)
```

Those errors are distributed **evenly** across the race text so that mistakes are spread throughout the race at regular intervals rather than clustered at the start or end.

Each intentional error uses a random character from the set `` ` ~ @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) `` — characters that never appear in NitroType race text. This means the cheat immediately knows to correct them, so every error is followed by an instant backspace-and-retype without any extra delay or configuration on your part.

At **100% accuracy** no errors are inserted. At **95% accuracy**, you get roughly **one error every 20 characters** — a rate that looks very natural in a real race.

<Tip>
  A setting of **92–98%** accuracy tends to look the most human. Anything higher than 99% over many races can appear suspicious, and anything much lower than 90% may hurt your race score noticeably.
</Tip>

## Accuracy Variance

The Accuracy Variance slider works the same way as Typing Rate Variance: it adds a random ± offset to your base accuracy at the start of each race. The effective accuracy per race is your base accuracy plus or minus a random value drawn from `[-variance, +variance]`, capped at a maximum of **100%**.

For example, with **Accuracy: 95** and **Accuracy Variance: 3**, each race runs at a randomly chosen accuracy between **92% and 98%**. This makes your error rate vary slightly from race to race, which is more consistent with how a real person's typing accuracy fluctuates.

<Note>
  If **God Mode** is active, accuracy is always forced to **100%** for that race regardless of what the Accuracy slider is set to. Your slider value is preserved and will resume as soon as you disable God Mode.
</Note>

## Saving Your Settings

All slider values are saved automatically when you close the app. Your Accuracy and Accuracy Variance settings will be restored the next time you launch NitroType Cheat v2.
