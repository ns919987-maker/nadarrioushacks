Welcome to the NitroType Cheat v2 documentation. NitroType Cheat v2 is a Windows desktop application that automates your typing in NitroType races, handling every keystroke so you can focus on winning. With fully configurable typing speed and accuracy simulation, you can reach up to approximately 4,000 WPM while still looking human — all in a single click. Version 4.6.3 ships with a suite of automation features including auto-start, auto-game, auto nitro usage, and a built-in ad blocker, with all your preferences saved automatically between sessions.

## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the Installer">
    Head to the GitHub releases page and download the latest version of the NitroType Cheat v2 installer for Windows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the Installer">
    Launch `nitroTypeInstaller.exe` and follow the on-screen prompts to complete the installation on your machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open NitroType Cheat">
    Once installation is complete, open the NitroType Cheat application. Your settings will be loaded automatically from the previous session, or defaults will be applied on first run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join a Race and Start">
    Navigate to NitroType in your browser, join a race, then click **Start** in the application — or enable **Auto Start** to have the cheat begin typing automatically as soon as each race countdown ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>
